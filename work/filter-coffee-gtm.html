CASE STUDY 02 · GO-TO-MARKET PLAYBOOK

Kaapi Kit: real filter coffee in 4 minutes

02

Getting instant-coffee drinkers to try real filter coffee, in 90 days.

Concept project: the brand is fictional, the thinking is mine.

  • BRANDKaapi Kit (fictional)
  • TYPEGo-to-market plan
  • MY ROLESegmentation, positioning, channel plan
  • TOOLSMeta Ads Manager · Google Analytics · Excel

01The brief

Kaapi Kit sells a ready-to-brew South Indian filter coffee kit: a ground blend plus a compact steel filter. It's launching in Pune and Mumbai, where most young professionals drink instant coffee and think filter coffee is "too much effort".

02Who it's for

The instant upgrader: 22–32, working, two or more cups a day, curious about better coffee but short on mornings. The barrier isn't taste or price. It's the belief that filter coffee is complicated.

03Positioning

"Real filter coffee in 4 minutes. No grandmother required."

Lead with the ritual being easy, not with heritage. Heritage is the reason to believe; speed is the reason to try.

04Offer & pricing logic

A 7-cup trial pack priced below two café coffees, so trying it feels obviously worth it. After the trial, a monthly refill subscription.

05The 90-day plan

  • Days 1–30 · Seed: Instagram reels of the 4-minute brew, morning-routine creators, sampling in co-working spaces.
  • Days 31–60 · Convert: Meta Ads retargeting video viewers, quick-commerce listings in both cities, search ads on "filter coffee at home".
  • Days 61–90 · Retain: a day-10 refill nudge, WhatsApp reminders, and a refer-a-friend trial pack.

06What I'd measure

Cost per trial, trial-to-subscription rate, 30-day repeat rate and CAC, with UTM-tagged links feeding Google Analytics so every channel has to earn its budget.
