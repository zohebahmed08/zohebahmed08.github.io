CASE STUDY 03 · CAMPAIGN AUDIT

Cuppa Corner's Instagram: keep, cut, test

03

"Instagram isn't bringing people in." So I audited it.

Concept project: the brand is fictional, the thinking is mine.

  • BRANDCuppa Corner (fictional)
  • TYPESocial & paid audit
  • MY ROLEAudit & recommendations
  • TOOLSMeta Ads Manager · Google Analytics · SEO · Excel

01The brief

Cuppa Corner is a neighbourhood café with about 4,000 Instagram followers. It posts every day and boosts a post now and then, but footfall hasn't moved. The owner's verdict: "Instagram doesn't work for us."

02How I audited it

  • Logged 90 days of posts in a sheet: format, topic, reach, saves, shares, profile visits and link clicks.
  • Compared boosted posts with what a proper Ads Manager campaign could target.
  • Checked what happens after the click: the menu link, the Google Business Profile, and whether any of it was tracked.

03The plan

KEEP

  • Behind-the-counter reels: latte art, the first batch of the day
  • Posts that feature regulars by name

CUT

  • Generic quote posts
  • Boosting random posts to the whole city
  • Posting daily just to post daily

TEST

  • Ads Manager campaigns in a 3 km radius, with an in-store offer
  • Reposting customer content
  • UTM-tagged menu link tracked in Google Analytics
  • Google Business Profile tidy-up for "café near me"

04What I'd measure

Offer redemptions in store (the closest thing to cost per visit), menu-link clicks in Google Analytics, and saves and shares per post instead of likes. Fewer posts, better posts, aimed at people who can actually walk in.
