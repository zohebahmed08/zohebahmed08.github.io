CASE STUDY 01 · BRAND REPOSITIONING PLAN

Bhaji Bros goes premium-casual

01

From cheap midnight snack to the pav bhaji place you can take anyone to.

Concept project: the brand is fictional, the thinking is mine.

  • BRANDBhaji Bros (fictional)
  • TYPEBrand repositioning
  • MY ROLEStrategy & positioning
  • TOOLSCanva · Excel · Google Maps reviews

01The brief

Bhaji Bros runs three pav bhaji outlets. People love the food after 11 pm, but nobody thinks of it before then. Weekday lunches are half-empty, customers pick it mainly because it's cheap, and delivery-first brands are winning the office crowd.

02What I looked at

  • Who actually comes: late-night students, a thin weekday lunch crowd, and families on weekends.
  • Competitor map: local players plotted on price vs. experience. There's a gap between roadside stalls and sit-down restaurants.
  • Review themes: sorted public reviews into praise (taste, butter, speed) and pain (seating, how clean it looks, waiting in the open).

03The repositioning

"Mumbai's comfort food, made properly."

Positioning statement: for working Mumbaikars who want a filling, feel-good meal, Bhaji Bros is the pav bhaji place you can take anyone to, because the kitchen is open, the butter is real and the tawa never stops.

  • Open tawa: turn the kitchen into the show. Visible cooking builds trust in hygiene.
  • Butter honesty: say how much butter goes in, and offer a "lite" option without apologising for it.
  • Anyone-welcome: seating and service you'd bring a colleague, a date or your parents to.

04The moves

  • A 30-minute office lunch combo, pushed through nearby offices and Google Business Profile.
  • A clearer menu: Classic, Cheese, Jain and Masala Lite, instead of 22 variations.
  • Refreshed storefront, packaging and Instagram built around tawa reels.

05What I'd measure

Weekday lunch covers, average order value, repeat visits, and the share of reviews that mention cleanliness or comfort. The goal isn't more butter. It's a better reason to choose Bhaji Bros at 1 pm.
